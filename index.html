The Story of How We Became Australian

This archive is dedicated to my two sons, Rory and Alexander. It traces our direct ancestry back to the very first person on every line to set foot on these shores, mapping the diverse paths that ultimately led to their lives here today.

Our family’s journey mirrors the very history of Australia itself. It is a tapestry woven from different eras, distinct motivations, and vastly different voyages. Within these pages, we document the convict arrivals who were forced onto the ships of the First Fleet, and the free settlers of the 1840s—some who risked everything to pay their own way, and others who arrived as assisted immigrants seeking a fresh start.

As the centuries turned, our lineage was shaped by arrivals from Europe in the late 19th and early 20th centuries, followed more recently by post-World War II migrant families determined to build a safer, brighter future. Completing the modern picture are the stories of the "Ten Pound Poms" who sought new horizons in a sunburnt country.

This project is more than just names and dates on a tree. It is an exploration of who our ancestors were, the social and historical forces that shaped their destinies, and the profound decisions—both chosen and forced—that map why and how we all ended up in Australia.
