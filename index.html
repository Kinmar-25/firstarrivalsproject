The Story of How We Became Australian

This website is dedicated to my two sons, Rory and Alexander. It traces our direct ancestry back to the very first person on every line to set foot on these shores, mapping the diverse paths that ultimately led to our lives here today.

Our family’s journey mirrors the very history of Australia itself. It is a tapestry woven from different eras, distinct motivations, and vastly different voyages. Within these pages, we document convict arrivals from the very first ships of the First Fleet, the arrival of Irish, Scottish and English free settlers in the mid-late 1800s— some of whom risked everything to pay their own way, and others who arrived as assisted immigrants seeking a fresh start.

Our lineage was further shaped by arrivals from Europe in the late 19th and early 20th centuries, followed more recently by families determined to build a safer, brighter future after World War II.

This project is more than just names and dates on a tree. It is an exploration of who our ancestors were, the social and historical forces that shaped their destinies, and the profound decisions — both chosen and forced — that map why and how we all ended up in Australia.
